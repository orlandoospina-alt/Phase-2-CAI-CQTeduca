How the Process Works

💻 Step 1: Digital Phase

Complete the computer-based listening and reading tasks. There is no writing in this section (45–60 mins).

🗣️ Step 2: Productive Phase

Participate in a 5 to 10-minute live speaking evaluation with an instructor, followed by a short writing task.

🎯 Step 3: Final Placement

Our expert teachers analyze your combined digital and communicative scores to assign your ideal level.
